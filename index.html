Tips

  • Seasonal vs per push: seasonal contracts pay the same no matter the snow, so they're safer in a light winter. Per push pays more in a heavy winter. Try both buttons above with a light and a heavy winter.
  • 2" trigger: most Holland services only come out at 2" or more. Write that into every deal so nobody expects you after a dusting.
  • Finish before school: customers want out by 7–8 AM. If the calculator says your route takes longer than you have, take fewer customers or keep them close together.
  • Sell the blower: unlike a plow truck, a snowblower throws the snow away, so it doesn't leave a ridge at the end of the driveway when the city plow comes by. Neighbors love that.
  • Get a real insurance quote before you sign anyone up. It's the number that changes the math the most.
  • Most seasonal contracts get signed in October. This late, expect more per-push and neighbor customers to start.
Where these numbers come from
  • Snowfall: Holland averages about 70" a year, about 23 days with 1"+ and about 8 days with 3"+ (NOAA 1981–2010 averages via Current Results and US Climate Data). The 14-push "average" winter is an estimate from those.
  • Local prices:
    • HoneyDid Holland: $45–95 per push, $350–800 per season
    • 87 Entertainment: $50 per push or $499 per season
    • Christian Brothers Outdoor: $50–100 per push, $350–650 per season
    • Prince: seasonal from $450
    • 2" trigger is standard: Advantage Landscape
  • Tractor price: $33,610 from the dealer's Marketplace listing (tractor + loader only; the snowblower is "call for price").
  • Estimates (marked estimate): snowblower price, freight, loan rate, fuel burn, driveways per hour, insurance, maintenance, marketing, and resale value. These are educated guesses, so change them when you get real numbers.
  • How the math works: fuel = customers ÷ driveways per hour × pushes × gallons per hour × diesel price. Payback = upfront cost ÷ profit before loan. Total over the years = profit before loan × years + resale − upfront cost − loan interest paid while you own it.
